How it works

Built on

Every handoff is real.

CrusoeAll model calls on open models via Crusoe Serverless Inference. DeepSeek-V4-Flash plans, GLM-5.3 writes, Qwen3.8-27B sees: it reads product screenshots and critiques rendered frames. Live per-job token and cost counter.
BandSix registered agents, one room per job. Handoffs are @mentions, the scout is recruited at runtime, the critic can veto, and rendering waits on a human approval in the room. Tool calls stream as room events. Delete the room and no agent receives work.
